Questions

Fair questions

Do I need a website?

No. For most trades, a strong Google listing with reviews does more than a website.

What do I have to do?

Confirm a code Google sends to your phone or email, and add us as a manager on your listing. About 2 minutes. We send step-by-step instructions.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay once, when you approve your preview. An optional upkeep plan exists if you want posts and review replies handled, but you never have to take it.

Can you guarantee more calls?

No one honest can. Google doesn’t let anyone pay for a better local ranking. What we guarantee is a complete, properly set-up profile and a simple way to collect reviews.

Who’s behind this?

A Drogheda-based business student who sets these up for local trades. Happy to meet in person.

What happens to my details?

We use them only to send your report and follow up about it. Say stop at any time and we won’t contact you again.
